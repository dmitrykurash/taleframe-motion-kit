# Included assets and source

This is a private working kit. Its source is not offered under a public open-source license.

- The reusable motion components, example films, and two SVG landscapes are included for use with the kit. The example products and their interface states are fictional.
- Taleframe transitions and effects are copied from the owner's local project. Their source records, file hashes, and adapter changes are listed in `src/taleframe/transitions/PROVENANCE.json`, `src/taleframe/effects/PROVENANCE.json`, and `tools/watercolor/PROVENANCE.json`. The source record distinguishes the local working snapshot from a committed release. Rendering does not require Taleframe to be installed.
- DM Sans and Instrument Serif are installed through Fontsource. Their font license files remain in those packages. Custom projects can load their own licensed fonts.
- Remotion, React, Lucide, Fontsource, and other dependencies keep their own licenses. See the installed packages for their terms; repository access does not replace any license your use of Remotion requires.

No real product's logos, proprietary typefaces, UI source, account data, or marketing backgrounds are included. Supply the assets for the product you want to demonstrate.
