# Included assets and source

This is a private working kit. Its source is not offered under a public open-source license.

- The reusable motion components, example films, and two SVG landscapes are included for use with the kit. The example products and their interface states are fictional.
- Taleframe transitions and effects are copied from the owner's local project. Their source records, file hashes, and adapter changes are listed in `src/taleframe/transitions/PROVENANCE.json`, `src/taleframe/effects/PROVENANCE.json`, and `tools/watercolor/PROVENANCE.json`. The source record distinguishes the local working snapshot from a committed release. Rendering does not require Taleframe to be installed.
- The oil style of the paint engine is kit code, recorded with its file hashes in `src/taleframe/effects/PROVENANCE.json`. Its stroke follows the method of the paper *Stylized Neural Painting* (Zou et al., CVPR 2021) and is implemented from the paper. That project's code, neural renderer checkpoints, and brush images are licensed CC BY-NC-SA; none of them is used or shipped here.
- The oil brushes in `public/taleframe/oil/brushes/` are procedural and the kit's own, drawn by `tools/oil/brushes.py`. No third-party brush image is used. `docs/images/oil.png` is a render of the kit's own SVG landscape.
- The three demo paintings in `public/demo/depth/` (a lake, a sitting room, a harbour) were generated for this kit with OpenAI image generation, through the Codex command-line tool in the ChatGPT desktop app. They show no people, animals, text, or logos. Their planes, fills, and tracks were cut with the kit's own tools in `tools/depth/`. The prompts and processing are recorded in `public/demo/depth/PROVENANCE.md`. `docs/images/painted-depth.jpg` is a frame of `Painted-Depth` rendered from those paintings.
- The model wrappers in `tools/depth/ml/` are kit code. They download models at run time into the git-ignored `.cache/models/`; no model weights are included. The models keep their upstream licenses: Depth Anything V2 Small (the default) is Apache-2.0, and Base and Large are CC BY-NC 4.0 (opt-in only); SAM 2.1 is Apache-2.0; BiRefNet is MIT, runs code from its model repository, and is pinned to one revision. The Python packages in `tools/depth/requirements.txt` keep their own licenses.
- DM Sans and Instrument Serif are installed through Fontsource. Their font license files remain in those packages. Custom projects can load their own licensed fonts.
- Remotion, React, Lucide, Fontsource, and other dependencies keep their own licenses. See the installed packages for their terms; repository access does not replace any license your use of Remotion requires.

No real product's logos, proprietary typefaces, UI source, account data, or marketing backgrounds are included. Supply the assets for the product you want to demonstrate.
